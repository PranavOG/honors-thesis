\section{Initial Stabilization of the Barrel}
\label{sec:init-stabilization}
The first test of the design involved understanding its stability in various salt conditions and temperatures to account for its use with conventional nanodiscs. The two halves were hybridized in \qty{5}{mM} \ce{MgCl2}, \qty{12.5}{mM} \ce{MgCl2}, \qty{20}{mM} \ce{MgCl2}, \qty{5}{mM} \ce{MgCl2} with \qty{150}{mM} \ce{NaCl} and \qty{5}{mM} \ce{MgCl2} with \qty{300}{mM} \ce{NaCl} at \qty{4}{\celsius}, \qty{15}{\celsius}, \qty{25}{\celsius} and \qty{40}{\celsius}. Monovalent counterions are used in conditions with low divalent ions as they help with stability.

[Figure Number] depicts the results of the salt and temperature scans for the unmodified barrels. The first lane is called a ladder, which contains samples of known bands that can be used to measure the migration of the samples. the second lane is the \qty{7560}{bp} scaffold used to build both half barrels. The [color] box in both gels are the excess single-stranded staples. The design is folded with 10 times more staples than the scaffold to encourage the probability a staple binding to its complementary portion of the scaffold. The black smear in each, called bands, does not show much information about the folding except that excess staples are present. One interesting feature is the shape of the smear on the staples. Here we see that the staples with a higher concentration of \ce{NaCl} lanes move slower (are higher up) in the gel than the others. This is because the sodium cations shielded the electric field used in gel electrophoresis.

The other bands are identified by how far they migrated. Correctly folded design should move slightly slower than the control scaffold band. This is because the design uses the same \qty{7560}{bp} scaffold and while more compact, has two scaffolds in the final structure. Example bands that are likely origamis are marked in green. If the design is well folded, it should present as a single dark and thin band. Streaking, secondary bands or a high amount of aggregated mass near the well of a lane indicates likely misfolded structures.
